What’s inside

Crash physics inspired by big simulators, optimized to run smoothly on mobile.

Soft-body vehicles

A 60–108-node beam network creates impact zones and permanent dents.

Flying wheels

Hit hard enough and wheels can break loose and roll away. Engines smoke and steering pulls to one side.

21 vehicle

From hatchbacks to tractor-trailers: police cars, ambulances, fire trucks, muscle cars and monster trucks.

City and test arena

A crash-test arena with ramps, plus a city with traffic, fences, poles and parking lots.

Night and rain

Day, evening or a bright moonlit night. Headlights, rain, emergency lights and sirens.

Coins and garage

Earn coins from every crash and unlock new vehicles in the garage.
